## Verdict: NEEDS REVISION

The core loop is built and is faithful to the v2 design on the phone: automatic general reveal, one-row draft with the slot reel and the consequence line, the pitch-and-inspector Deploy with live cohesion and the reserve tag, a beat-stepped match report with the Key/Every toggle, the between-battles screen, resume from `localStorage`, and the Rules and Numbers pages with the artboard copy. Palette v2 tokens, stat words and the "no cost, no `?` boxes on Deploy" decisions are all honoured. Nothing here needs a redesign.

It is not ready to call approved because five things a first-time phone player will hit are unhandled: the player who loses gets the thinnest explanation in the app; on a 667px-tall phone the primary button can sit below the fold on Draft and Deploy; every run screen is a blank dark rectangle while the engine loads and there is no error state at all; the small-type contrast, motion and target-size choices fail the Standard tier the spec implies; and the draft stays editable after battles are fought, which silently rewrites past results. Each has a concrete, contained fix. No item requires a backend.

Blocking: 5 (the Top 5 below). Advisory: the rest.

## Top 5 findings, ranked by player impact

### 1. The losing player gets the least explanation in the app

**Impact:** the whole learning loop of a roguelite daily ("why did I lose, what do I change tomorrow") depends on this, and the spec (§3.5, §6) is explicit that the battle should show who did what.

**Evidence**

- `apps/web/src/app/battle/[battle]/page.tsx:169–189` renders one opposed bar per contest with only the front key (`L`, `C`, `L→C`), a `+31%`/`−28%` tag, and nothing else. The spec's `topA`/`topB` unit names, the damage line (`YOU TAKE +0.28` / `HE TAKES +0.19` / `NOTHING LOST`) and the morale delta (§4.5) are absent. The engine already returns `topA`, `topB`, `damageA`, `damageB` on every `ContestRecord` (`packages/engine/src/beats.ts:21`).
- `apps/web/src/lib/battleView.ts:23` writes the edge tag as `+31%` / `−31%`; the spec (§4.5) says `YOURS BY 31%` / `HIS BY 28%`. A plus/minus sign with colour is not a readable "who won" for a new player, and the sign is the only non-colour cue.
- The chronicle ("HOW IT WENT", one clause per beat) is rendered only on the win-only Between screen (`apps/web/src/app/between/[battle]/page.tsx:73–81`). The loss path skips Between and goes to Result, where each battle gets just the final headline sentence and the shapes (`apps/web/src/app/result/page.tsx:105–126`). So the winner reads a beat-by-beat account and the loser reads one sentence.
- Result's headline on a loss is a fixed template ("Your wings went, and the line would not stand alone.", `packages/engine/src/beats.ts:120–123`) with no pointer to the front, unit or stage that decided it.

**v2.1 note.** The working revision drops the unit names and the damage line from the beat card and standardises on `+31%`/`HELD`, so on these two points the build now matches the newer spec. That does not answer the player's question; it moves the gap from the code to the spec. The recommendation stands for both: the names and damage are the only per-beat facts that connect a loss to a draft or deployment choice, and they cost nothing to show because the engine already emits them. If the compact card is kept for "Every beat", put the names and damage on the lifted key-beat cards at least, and give the loser the chronicle on Result.

**Fix (can build now)**

- In the contest card, print `topA[0].name` under the left half and `topB[0].name` under the right half of each bar (13px sans, dim), then a mono line `YOU TAKE +0.28 · HE TAKES +0.19`. Data is already on `c`.
- Change `opposedBars()` to emit `YOURS BY n%` / `HIS BY n%` / `HELD` (v2), or at minimum `YOU +31%` / `HIM +28%` if v2.1's compact tag is kept; a bare sign is not a name.
- On Result, render the same "HOW IT WENT" list per battle (you already compute `beatsBy`), and on a loss add a one-line "what decided it" taken from `decisiveClause(beats)` plus the front nearest its threshold at the break (`frontsAfter` gives the fraction).

### 2. The primary button can fall below the fold on shorter phones

**Impact:** on a 375×667 phone (iPhone SE / 8 class, still common) the Draft and Deploy CTAs, which are the only way forward, are off-screen until the player discovers the page scrolls. The spec's phone frame is 390×844; nothing in the code pins the bar for anything shorter.

**Evidence**

- `apps/web/src/components/Screen.tsx:5` is `flex min-h-dvh flex-col` and `apps/web/src/components/BottomBar.tsx:5` is `shrink-0` inside it. Neither is `sticky`/`fixed`, so once content exceeds the viewport the bar scrolls with the page.
- Draft height: header 52 (`RunHeader.tsx:9`) + step bar ≈ 15 + row header ≈ 70 (`draft/[row]/page.tsx:132–149`) + 4 cards × `min-h-[132px]` (`:156`) + 3 × 9 gap + bottom bar ≈ 150 (three chips, note, button, safe-area) ≈ 843px. That is the 844 target exactly; on 667px the CTA is ~175px below the fold.
- Deploy: header 52 + title row ≈ 64 + field `min-h-[250px]` (`deploy/[battle]/page.tsx:116`) + inspector `h-[142px]` (`:154`) + bench ≈ 78 + bottom bar (pills + note + button) ≈ 160 ≈ 750px. Below the fold on 667.
- Battle keeps the controls visible only because `scrollIntoView` runs on every beat (`battle/[battle]/page.tsx:51–53`); on first paint at 667px the Play/Next row is still off-screen behind the pinned morale header, front tiles and toggle (≈ 300px before the first beat).

**Fix (can build now)**

- Make `BottomBar` `position: sticky; bottom: 0` (and the Battle controls row, which is hand-rolled at `battle/[battle]/page.tsx:222`). Give `Screen` `h-dvh` with `main` as `overflow-y-auto min-h-0` so the header and bar stay put and only the middle scrolls.
- Drop the card `min-h-[132px]` to 118px on `max-height: 700px` screens (the six-stat grid still fits) and let the Deploy field's `min-h` be 200 at that breakpoint.

### 3. Blank screen while loading, and no error state anywhere

**Impact:** the first thing a new player sees on any run route, on a slow connection, is a solid `#100f0c` rectangle with no text. A failed data fetch or a version mismatch throws and the app dies with Next's default error page.

**Evidence**

- Every run page early-returns `<Screen />` with no children while `engine`, `save` or hydration is missing: `general/page.tsx:33`, `draft/[row]/page.tsx:85`, `draft/board/page.tsx:17`, `deploy/[battle]/page.tsx:66`, `deploy/[battle]/roster/page.tsx:18`, `battle/[battle]/page.tsx:51`, `between/[battle]/page.tsx:32`, `result/page.tsx:31`.
- Today shows `SEED …` and a disabled "Draft the army" at 60% opacity (`page.tsx:91, 107`) with no word that says "loading".
- `apps/web/src/lib/engine/EngineProvider.tsx:55` rethrows load errors from `useEngine()`; there is no `error.tsx`, `global-error.tsx` or `loading.tsx` anywhere under `apps/web/src/app` (checked with `find`). `CampaignProvider.tsx:58` calls `useEngine()` at the top of the tree, so the throw takes the whole app down, including `/rules` and `/numbers`, which do not need the engine.
- The Deploy inspector's empty state and the draft's "fields only N units" note are the only true empty states; there is no state for a corrupt or older `wd.campaign.v1` save (a schema change will throw inside `resolveAll` at `CampaignProvider.tsx:48` on `replayDraft`).

**Fix (can build now)**

- Replace the bare `<Screen />` with a `Screen` containing the run header and a mono line `MUSTERING THE ARMY…` (`aria-live="polite"`), so the frame paints before the data.
- Add `apps/web/src/app/error.tsx` (bone button "Try again", secondary "Back to today") and make `EngineProvider` expose `{state:"error"}` to a small banner instead of throwing; wrap `storage.loadSave()` in a `v === 1` check that discards and reports an unreadable save ("Your saved campaign was from an older version and has been cleared").

Other accessibility findings:

- **Motion.** No `prefers-reduced-motion` anywhere (`grep` over `apps/web/src` returns nothing). The reel runs 1.5s per row, eight rows a run plus every reroll (`draft/[row]/page.tsx:42–58`, `globals.css:88–96`), the general flip is 560ms × 3 (`general:81`), each beat rises (`battle:152`), and `scrollIntoView({behavior:"smooth"})` fires on every beat (`battle:52`). Under reduced motion the reel should settle in one frame, the flip become a fade, and scroll be `auto`.
- **Targets under 44px** (spec §2.4): doctrine pills `min-h-9` = 36px (`deploy:224`); Result "Back to today" / "New free run" `min-h-9` (`result:152, 156`); "Abandon this free campaign" is a bare text button ≈ 16px tall (`page.tsx:99`); "Close" on Roster (`roster:24`), "Numbers" on Rules (`rules:48`) and "Rules" on Numbers (`numbers:57`) are `px-2` links with no height; the `?` link is 44×40 (`RunHeader.tsx:16`); Board-sheet "Close" is `py-2` text (`draft/board/page.tsx:40`).
- **Disabled buttons hide their reason.** Elite-capped draft cards use the `disabled` attribute (`UnitCard.tsx:17`), so keyboard and screen-reader users cannot focus them and never hear "ELITE CAP FULL — DROP ONE TO TAKE THIS". Use `aria-disabled="true"` and keep the click a no-op.
- **No live regions.** Beats appear on a timer, the Deploy inspector changes on tap, "Copied" flashes for 1.6s (`result:58`); none of it is announced. Add `aria-live="polite"` on the beat list container, the inspector, and the copy feedback.
- **Focus.** No `:focus-visible` style is defined; the browser default ring is what shows on a near-black ground. It is visible but off-brand and, on the rotateY general cards, is drawn on the un-rotated box. Define `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--bone); outline-offset: 2px }` once in `globals.css`.
- **Text scaling.** Every size is an absolute `text-[7px]`…`text-[15px]`; the stat labels are 7px (`StatGrid.tsx:9`, `deploy:180`, `battle:112, 121`). 7px mono uppercase with 0.06em tracking is below any legibility floor on a 390px phone and does not respond to iOS Dynamic Type. Raise the floor to 9px and express labels in `rem` so browser zoom carries them.
- **Semantics that are right and should stay:** `<main>`, `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<h1>` per page, `role="progressbar"` with values on `StepBar`, `aria-pressed` on cards/tokens/pills, `aria-label` on icon links, real `<button>`/`<a>` everywhere, `lang="en"`.

**Fix (can build now):** raise `--faint-2` to `#857D6C` (≈4.5:1 on panel) or restrict it to ≥13px; replace the `#5c5749` literals with `--faint`; render 0 stats as `--faint` with a `—`; set the general-card culture label in bone or add a 1px inset; add the reduced-motion block; bring the six sub-44px controls to `min-h-11`; switch disabled cards to `aria-disabled`.

### 5. Orientation gaps and a state-integrity hole in the run flow

**Impact:** the player does not always know where they are in the campaign, and can (rarely, via browser Back) change a draft that battles have already been fought with.

**Evidence**

- The six-segment campaign bar (`lib/steps.ts`, spec §3 "at the top of every run screen") is rendered only on General (`general:51`), Between (`between:46`) and, as the eight-row variant, Draft (`draft:130`). Deploy, Battle and Result have none, so from "Set the line" onward the only progress cue is the 11px header label.
- Deploy's header reads `Battle n of 3 · The field` (`deploy:95`); `docs/mvp-plan.md` §3 asks for `BATTLE 2 OF 3 · HILLS` plus the enemy general's name. The ground is only in an 8px mono line inside the field (`deploy:119`) and the foe's name is only on the Roster sub-page. The player is choosing a doctrine and a line without the two facts that should drive it in view.
- Row 1's Back goes to `/general` (`draft:120`), which immediately bounces to `/draft/{row}` because the general is committed (`general:25`). The arrow does nothing and gives no reason.
- The draft row page guards only "no save" and "no general" (`draft/[row]/page.tsx:29–32`). After battle 1 is fought, `/draft/8` is still in browser history (Deploy's Back points there, `deploy:94`) and still accepts `pickCard`/`unpickRow`. `CampaignProvider.tsx:44–53` recomputes every fought battle from the draft, so a changed pick silently rewrites battle 1's result, the history entry already pushed at `:157`, and the share card.
- The end-of-battle CTA reads "The reckoning" (`battle:229`) for a won battle 1 or 2. That is engine vocabulary (`endedLabel` maps `break` to `RECKONING`); a new player has no idea what it opens. The mock says the next step plainly.

**Fix (can build now):** add `<StepBar steps={STEPS} current={2+i}>` to Deploy and Battle and `current={5}` to Result; put `BATTLE 2 OF 3 · HILLS · vs SURENA` in the Deploy header label; hide the Back arrow on row 1 (or route it to the Board sheet); in the draft page, if any `save.battles[k].fought`, render the row read-only with a note "The army is set; battles have been fought with it" and route Back from Deploy 1 to `/draft/board`; rename the CTA to "On to battle 2" / "Battle 2: Surena of Persia".

## Accessibility summary

- **Contrast:** primary and secondary text pass comfortably (bone 14–16:1, dim 6.5–8.5:1, faint 4.8–6.3:1). Fails: `--faint-2` (2.65–3.47:1) used on 8–11px text on every screen; `#5c5749` literals (2.03–2.66:1); `--zero` (1.5–2:1) for 0-valued stats; grade B chip 4.37:1; culture bright on own deep 3.13–4.31:1 for Rome, Carthage, Macedon, India, Gauls; rust on raised 4.05:1 and on dimmed cards. Non-text: `--rule` borders on panel are 1.46:1 and `--rule-btn` on ground 2.01:1, under the 3:1 non-text minimum for outlined buttons (OutlineButton, reroll, doctrine pills, RUN).
- **Colour-only information:** cohesion (word + number + colour) and grades (letter + lightness) are fine. Battle opposed bars: you-vs-him is hue-only with equal lightness by design; mitigated by position and the name columns, not by the tag. Draft culture edge is decorative. Selected doctrine has the rust edge plus raised fill and text weight: fine.
- **Target size:** most controls are ≥44px; the exceptions are listed in Top 5 #4.
- **Focus & keyboard:** all interactive elements are real buttons/links in DOM order that matches reading order; no traps; the Deploy overlays appear after the tokens, which is the right order. No custom focus ring; no skip link (single-column pages, so low priority).
- **Screen reader:** landmarks and `h1` present on every page; `StepBar` is a proper progressbar; no live regions; disabled cards unreachable; the general card's accessible name concatenates both faces (acceptable). The check glyph `✓` is text, fine.
- **Motion:** no reduced-motion handling for reel, flip, rise, or smooth scroll.
- **Text scaling:** all px; 7px labels in three places.

## Lower-priority notes

- Copy: "The reckoning" (`battle:229`) and `RECKONING` in the ENDED tile (`battleView.ts:62`) are engine words; say "Battle 2 of 3" and "BREAK".
- Copy: the Today card says `ABOUT 6 MIN`; the Rules page still says "Four steps, three times over" but the header line under the muster says nothing about length in words. Fine, but keep one number.
- Copy: STEADY collision (stat vs cohesion word), see Deploy row.
- `Screen`'s `text-bone` plus `a { color: var(--link) }` means any link that forgets `no-underline text-…` turns orange (`#e08a5f`, a fourth accent the palette does not allow). Every current link overrides it; make the base `a` inherit instead.
- `Token.tsx:15` has `text-center` twice; `deploy:184` colours 75+ stats white but the draft `statColor` does the same at ≥75 while the general card uses ≥85 (`general:109`): pick one threshold.
- The general reveal keeps the ordinal "THE FIRST" only on the face-down side; once flipped the three cards have no index, so the footer hint "Tap the one you want" cannot refer to them by position. Minor.
- Draft chips show `TAKEN 5 / 8` in `--center` (a class tint) rather than bone; it reads as a wing/centre hint.
- The Deploy field's centre circle (`deploy:117`) sits behind the CENTER front's tokens on narrow widths; purely decorative, but it is the one gradient-free flourish and it collides with content at 375px.
- `formatMusterDate` in `daily.ts` is unused by Today (which uses its own `longDate`); dead code.
- Reels draw names from "the row's culture and two others" (`draft:73–83`), which matches §5.1, but the `others[(rowIndex*2) % …]` pick means row 4 and row 8 spin the same two foreign cultures; harmless.
- `history` is keyed by campaign id, so a free run with the same random seed twice would overwrite; negligible.
- Result's share line 1 uses `fmtDate(...).slice(4)` to strip the weekday; `fmtDate` returns `WED 16 SEP` so `.slice(4)` works only for three-letter weekdays. Fine in `en-US`.
